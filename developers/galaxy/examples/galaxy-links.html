Links that open Galaxy at the right place

Change any box and the links change with it. Nothing is sent to Galaxy until someone clicks a link.

1. Open a tool
2. Import a workflow and open its run form

Students must log in before they click. Galaxy sends a student who is logged out to its login page, and forgets the workflow.

3. Import a file from a web address

A plain link cannot carry the file's address. Run this once in a terminal to store a "landing request" on the server. It prints an ID: share /tool_landings/ID?public=true on that server. Landing requests cannot be deleted.
